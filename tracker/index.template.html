Already in HOME

HOME origin

The real permanents. Park one back to HOME to free the slot and recall it later with its moves, nature, ability and SP intact — free, as often as you like. Only these make the box elastic.

Champions origin

Came out of an Encounter, whether bought for 2500 VP or with a ticket. It can never be sent to HOME. The slot frees only by releasing it, which destroys the Pokemon.

Rental

Timed, 0 VP, cannot be trained. Always Champions origin — buying one does not make it a real permanent.

HOME Box

Waiting outside Champions. Arrives permanent and trainable, 0 VP — the only route that delivers a chosen species on purpose.

GTS offers open

Three slots, and an open offer holds one until it is taken or withdrawn. A deposit is not a trade: until someone accepts it, the Pokemon is still yours — and parked, so it cannot be sent to Champions.
